How ratings work

Each team's rating is its average result plus the average rating of the teams it played. Because opponents' ratings depend on their own opponents, the page repeats the calculation until the numbers settle.

Rating = AGD + Sched
AGD = each game's result, based on goal margin (capped at 10), averaged over the season
Sched = average rating of opponents

Ratings are relative, so a gap of 1.0 between two teams means roughly a one-goal edge. A game is skipped when two teams are more than 10 goals apart and the stronger team wins by 10 or more.
